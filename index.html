Model scope

This is a deliberately simplified, steady-state model of one collapsible airway with a logistic tube law and linear upstream dissipative resistance. Airway width represents displayed diameter proportional to √A(Ptm); it does not represent anatomical airway branching. The simulator is intended for physiological education, not clinical prediction.

Based on Dawson and Elliott’s wave-speed formulation and Pedersen & Butler, Expiratory Flow Limitation, Comprehensive Physiology 2011. doi:10.1002/cphy.c100025
